Policies

70% booking deposit

A 70% deposit is required to secure your appointment. This deposit is part of your total session price, not an additional charge.

Booking confirmation

Your booking is confirmed only after payment has been received and a confirmation message has been sent to you.

Remaining balance

The remaining 30% balance must be paid before your massage session begins.

Cancellations and rescheduling

Please provide at least 24 hours notice if you need to cancel or reschedule. With proper notice, your deposit may be transferred once to another available appointment within 30 days.

Late cancellations and missed appointments

Deposits may be forfeited for cancellations made with less than 24 hours notice or for missed appointments without notice.

Health first

Please disclose any medical conditions, allergies, injuries, pregnancy, recent surgery, or medications that could affect your treatment.

Confidentiality

our personal and booking information will remain confidential except when disclosure is required by law.

Your agreement

By paying the 70% deposit, you confirm that you have read, understood, and accepted this booking policy.
